Privacy Policy

Effective 24 September 2026

In short: Kolam a Day collects no personal data. It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no internet access. Everything it keeps stays on your phone.

Kolam a Day (“the app”) teaches sikku kolam: each day it shows one design that you trace with your finger. This policy explains what the app stores and where that information goes. The app is made by Shamala Mallya, an independent developer in Bengaluru, India.

What the app stores on your phone

  • Your practice history: which designs you traced, on which days, and how long each took. The app uses this for your streak and the Practice screen.
  • Your settings: line and dot colours, strict mode, and whether music plays.
  • Pictures you share: when you tap Share, the app draws a picture of the kolam into its temporary cache so another app can send it.

This information never leaves your phone through the app. The app has no internet permission, so it cannot send anything anywhere.

What the app does not do

  • It does not collect your name, email address, contacts, location or photos.
  • It has no accounts or sign-in.
  • It shows no ads and contains no analytics, tracking or crash-reporting tools.
  • It asks for no permissions.
  • It does not sell or share any data, because it has none to share.

Backups by Android

If you have turned on Android backup, your phone may copy the app’s practice history and settings to your Google account, so they come back when you set up a new phone. Android and Google do this, not the app, under Google’s privacy policy. The temporary share pictures are never included. You can turn backup off in your phone’s settings.

Sharing

Sharing happens only when you tap Share. You then choose the app that sends the picture, such as a messaging app, and that app’s own privacy policy applies to what it does with the picture.

Deleting your information

To delete everything the app has stored, uninstall it, or go to Settings → Apps → Kolam a Day → Storage and clear its storage.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and it collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If the app’s handling of information changes, this page will be updated and the effective date above will change.
